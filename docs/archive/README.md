## Interpretation

- `acceptance/`: immutable owner-machine and phase validation evidence; a passed subtest does not imply a later full feature was accepted.
- `architecture/`: owner-approved or proposed designs from the phase at that time, not automatic current running architecture.
- `implementation/`: original phased execution plans, completion notes and slices.
- `research/`: hypotheses and technology research from previous decisions; may have been superseded.
- `cost/`, `operations/`, `handovers/`: period-specific observations, decisions and handover records.
- `../reference/`: supporting approved/planned or shadow technical designs retained for future engineering work; explicitly check status.

**Note:** Historical archive file contents are retained byte-for-byte, including old relative references to their former paths. For relocated targets, consult the table above. Avoid editing archived evidence in place; record new decisions in current canonical documents.
